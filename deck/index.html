01 · The situation

One great trade, one unhedged problem

Friday 20 Nov was a broad risk-off session: S&P 500 −3.7%, NDX −5.6%, VIX +26% to 35.89, NVDA −14%, AMD −13%.

The account made +$58,740 on an NVDA 172.50 put. But the put was ITM at expiry and was auto-exercised into an inferred short of ~2,600 NVDA shares.

The AMD 462.50 call expired worthless: −$2,770. Two lessons, both now hard rules.

Monday, before any trade

  • Buy to cover the NVDA short (unbounded risk).
  • Confirm the expired AMD call is gone.
  • Verify the settled position list and cash.

Until this is done, the agent returns RECONCILE for every idea. No exceptions.

Cash

07 · What worked, what failed

Four failures that became four rules

Failed

  • Holding a deep-ITM option to expiry → assignment into short stock.
  • Concentrated AI single names → −14% / −13% in one day.
  • Cheap OTM call → $2,770 to zero.
  • No reconciliation before expiry → the account was unknown at the worst time.

Changed

  • Reconcile-first gate; no option held into expiry.
  • Universe restricted to SPY/QQQ/IWM; NVDA/AMD blocked.
  • Premium budget buys defined-risk spreads only.
  • Daily reconcile, append-only log, kill switch.
